Corporate Profile

About Dispoline India

Incorporated in 2006, Dispoline India Private Limited is a Bangalore-based manufacturer of sterile, single-use medical disposables. For over 20 years we have served hospitals, surgery centres and government health programmes with customised procedure kits, surgical gowns, drapes and PPE — all manufactured under a certified Quality Management System and validated sterilization process.

Our Mission

Delivering quality healthcare disposables that create value.

Our mission is to provide the healthcare industry with quality disposables that are cost-effective, increase safety and aid productivity. Why It Matters

Benefits Built Into Every Batch

Infection Prevention & Patient Safety

Single-use sterile products eliminate cross-contamination risk and protect both patient and surgical team from healthcare-associated infections.

Reduced Cost & Time Saving

Customised procedure kits eliminate the time and cost of assembling individual items. Faster theatre turnaround and reduced nursing preparation time.

Bundled kits reduce SKU count, storage requirements and capital tied up in loose stock. Fewer purchase orders, less administration.
