Corporate Profile

About Dispoline India

For over a decade, Dispoline India has manufactured sterile, single-use medical disposables from our ISO 13485-certified facility. We serve tertiary hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers and government health missions across India and 20+ export markets, engineering every product to a zero-defect standard.

Our Mission

Form follows function — always.

We design for the operating theatre first: sequential unpacking, fail-safe sterility indicators, and materials chosen for clinical performance, not cosmetics. Every facility decision — from cleanroom class to batch traceability — is made to protect the patient outcome at the end of the supply chain.
